Company FAQ

1. What is the return policy?
Customers can return products within 30 days of delivery
if the product is unused and meets the company's return conditions.

2. How can I track my order?
Customers can track orders using the tracking ID provided
in the order confirmation email.

3. How can I contact support?
Customers can contact support through email or the
customer service portal.

4. What payment methods are supported?
The company supports credit cards, debit cards, and other
payment methods configured at checkout.

5. How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery arrives within 5 to 7 business days.
Express delivery arrives within 2 business days.

6. Can I change the delivery address after ordering?
The delivery address can be changed while the order status
is still Processing. Once the order has shipped, the address
cannot be changed.
