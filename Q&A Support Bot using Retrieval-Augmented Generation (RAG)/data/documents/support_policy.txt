Customer Support Policy

Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.
Requests received outside these hours are answered on the next business day.

Response targets
Standard priority requests receive a first response within 24 hours.
High priority requests receive a first response within 4 hours.
A request is treated as high priority when an order has already shipped
incorrectly or when a payment has been charged twice.

Refund processing
Once a returned item has been received and inspected, the refund is issued
to the original payment method. The refund reaches the customer within
5 to 10 business days depending on the card issuer.

Damaged or incorrect items
A damaged or incorrect item must be reported within 7 days of delivery.
Photographs of the item and the packaging are required before a replacement
is dispatched. Replacements are sent at no additional cost to the customer.

Order cancellation
An order can be cancelled without charge while its status is Processing.
After the order has shipped, the standard return process applies instead
of cancellation.

Escalation
A customer may request escalation to a support supervisor at any point.
Escalated requests receive a response within 8 working hours.

Account and data requests
A customer may request a copy of their account data or ask for their account
to be closed by contacting support through the customer service portal.
Identity verification is required before either request is processed.
